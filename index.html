2 · Reference points

The floor should lie flat on the grid. If the room is upside down or on its side, change this.

Click a room corner where it meets the floor, then a point on the floor along one of its walls, 1.5 m or more away. Remember which corner and wall: you will touch the same ones in the headset.

Optional scale check: click two points you can measure in the real room (e.g. a door width), enter the real distance, then Set scale.

These settings are saved on this device and in the page link. If you prepare on a computer, open the link on the Quest: copy it, or show a QR code and scan it.
